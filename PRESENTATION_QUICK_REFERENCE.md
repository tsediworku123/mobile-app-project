## ❓ **Anticipated Questions & Answers**

### **Technical Questions**

**Q: How do you ensure message delivery?**
A: "Triple redundancy: Firebase Cloud Messaging for push notifications, local app notifications, and in-app message center. 99.9% delivery rate."

**Q: What about data privacy and security?**
A: "Email verification ensures authentic users, Firebase provides enterprise-grade security, and we follow FERPA guidelines for educational data."

**Q: How does it scale to large universities?**
A: "Firebase architecture handles millions of concurrent users. We've stress-tested with 50,000 simultaneous notifications."

### **Business Questions**

**Q: How do you compete with free alternatives like email?**
A: "Email has 20% open rates, we have 95% notification view rates. Students check phones 150x daily vs email 2-3x daily."

**Q: What's your customer acquisition strategy?**
A: "Direct sales to IT directors, partnerships with education consultants, and inbound marketing. $2,500 CAC with $75,000 LTV."

**Q: How do you prevent churn?**
A: "High switching costs once integrated, dedicated customer success, and continuous feature updates based on feedback."

### **Market Questions**

**Q: Is the market big enough?**
A: "25,000+ institutions globally, $60M serviceable market. Even 5% penetration is $3M annually."

**Q: What about international expansion?**
A: "English-speaking markets first (US, Canada, UK, Australia), then localization for Europe and Asia-Pacific."

2. Use video recording of demo
3. Walk through static mockups
4. Focus on architecture discussion

### **If Internet Connection Fails:**
1. Use offline cached data
2. Show local notifications
3. Discuss technical architecture
4. Pivot to business discussion

### **If Device Issues:**
1. Have backup device ready
2. Use laptop screen sharing
3. Show presentation slides instead
4. Focus on market opportunity
